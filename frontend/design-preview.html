Why did the professor use that long formula?

The formula separates the part into two volumes:

  1. the complete outer shape;
  2. the empty cavity inside it.
Material volume = outer volume − inner cavity

This is longer than multiplying one area by height, but it correctly preserves the 3.73 mm wall thickness.
