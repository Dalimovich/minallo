A CLEARER WAY TO LEARN

Your work.
Your judgement.

AI can make mistakes. Check explanations and generated study material against your original sources. Minallo supports learning; it does not guarantee grades, exam success, or official language certification.

What can I bring?

Use lecture PDFs, scripts, slides, formula sheets and exercise sheets in a course workspace. Readable source material helps; poor scans and complex diagrams may need checking.

No. These public examples are scripted samples running in your browser. They do not upload documents or call AI services. The signed-in product works with your own learning material.
